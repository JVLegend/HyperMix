# The preprint starts with what failed

The first internal manuscript draft for HyperMix now exists. It does not begin
with a neural network victory because the benchmark did not produce one.

Instead, the draft is organized around information contracts. Every learned
method is compared with the strongest classical baseline allowed to use the
same target knowledge. Training, calibration, and evaluation implants are
separated. Scene and seed cases, rather than neighboring pixels, define the
bootstrap units.

The central table records seven completed experimental axes. Background
learning and calibrated uncertainty were significantly worse than the spatial
matched filter. The abundance MAE difference was inconclusive, while the
learned intervals were significantly wider. The broad physical target family
failed its primary criterion even though a narrow nominal transform improved
the laboratory signature in a declared secondary analysis.

The manuscript also gives the blocked real-target reproduction its own
section. The candidate boxes do not reproduce the nine Figure 4g regional
scores, so no detector comparison is reported on that biological target. A
missing coordinate bridge is not silently converted into a positive result.

This draft is still internal and in Portuguese. Translation and submission
come later, after an external reproduction and a final decision on the missing
Figure 4g geometry. The goal of writing now is to expose gaps while they are
still cheap to fix.
